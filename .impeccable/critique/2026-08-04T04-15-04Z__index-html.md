- The three-column home composition teaches checks, reading sequence, and retained human authority through layout rather than decoration.
- Provenance is first-class: source path, Markdown/GitHub links, active source, and local outline support the evidence-led promise.

## Priority Issues

### [P1] Full-corpus ledger dominates the desktop reader

- **Why it matters:** Beginners face two navigation systems plus the article before they secure the mental model.
- **Fix:** Default to category summaries/current category and reveal all 51 documents on demand while preserving direct lookup within three actions.
- **Suggested command:** `$impeccable distill`

### [P1] Invalid deep links fail silently

- **Why it matters:** Falling back to home makes a stale shared URL look intentional and undermines source authority.
- **Fix:** Render a compact not-found state with the requested path, README/home recovery, and closest category.
- **Suggested command:** `$impeccable harden`

### [P2] Mobile process context precedes every document title

- **Why it matters:** Returning readers must cross the same stacked orientation block before content.
- **Fix:** Keep the work-map link and current phase in one row; reveal the full chain on demand.
- **Suggested command:** `$impeccable adapt`

### [P2] Three simultaneous six-item narratives weaken the home CTA

- **Why it matters:** First-timers compare columns instead of simply beginning.
- **Fix:** Make `1 처음 이해하기` the unmistakable primary action and subordinate checks/decisions without losing the mental model.
- **Suggested command:** `$impeccable layout`

### [P2] Korean-first copy still mixes unexplained operator jargon

- **Why it matters:** `authority`, `runtime`, `grilling`, and `trade-off` impose translation work before comprehension.
- **Fix:** Put Korean first and attach a short definition or glossary link on first occurrence.
- **Suggested command:** `$impeccable clarify`
